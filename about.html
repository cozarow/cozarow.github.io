hey! i'm cerulean ozarow/səˈɹuːli.ən ˈɔːzəɹoʊ/. i currently work as a middle and high school math teacher at hunter college high school in nyc. i'm also the co-director of the organizing committee for NACLO (a linguistics competition for high school students in which i finished in the top 5 nationally in 2020), a freelance writer, and a private tutor. when i'm not doing those things, i'm probably making music, reading, answering trivia questions, exploring, or learning something new.

in the past i've been a quantitative trading intern at jane street, a participant in the ARBOx ai safety bootcamp, and a fulbright ETA grantee (declined), among other exploits. i graduated from brown in 2025 with an sc.b. in applied mathematics; my thesis was on school zoning optimization in nyc. if you'd like a full résumé, or want to reach out about anything you think i'd be interested in, feel free to contact me here!

this website is intended as a repository for various personal projects of mine. it's also been a way to learn a bit about how to code a website! hope you enjoy :)
