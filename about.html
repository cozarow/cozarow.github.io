hey! i'm cerulean ozarow/səˈɹuːli.ən ˈɔːzəɹoʊ/. i currently work as a middle and high school math teacher at hunter college high school in nyc. i'm also the co-director of the organizing committee for NACLO (a linguistics competition for high school students in which i finished in the top 5 nationally in 2020), a freelance writer, and a private tutor.

this summer i enjoyed participating in the ARBOx ai safety bootcamp. last summer i received a fulbright grant to teach english abroad over the past year (declined), and two summers ago i worked as a quantitative trading intern at jane street. three summers ago i taught at BEAM, and four summers ago i conducted research in language policies in education with prof. pierre de galbert. in between, i learned about everything from manichaean cosmology to modern scientific cosmology at brown university, whence i graduated in 2025 with an sc.b. in applied mathematics; my thesis was on school zoning optimization in nyc. for a full résumé, or to reach out about anything you think i'd be interested in, feel free to contact me here!
